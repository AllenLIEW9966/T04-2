About EnergyWatch

Project Overview & Background

Empowering consumers with accessible, data-driven appliance energy insights.

Our Mission

EnergyWatch was developed as part of the COS30045 Data Visualisation unit at Swinburne University of Technology. Our mission is to translate complex, raw appliance registration datasets into clear, actionable data stories that assist Australian households in making cost-effective and environmentally conscious decisions.

Generative AI Declaration

In accordance with university assessment guidelines, generative AI tools were used during development:

  • Tool: ChatGPT was used as an editorial assistant for drafting semantic HTML structures, refining CSS layouts, and reviewing storytelling narratives.
  • Student Responsibility: All KNIME data cleaning workflows, data aggregations, chart generation, and final code reviews were authored, tested, and validated by the student.
